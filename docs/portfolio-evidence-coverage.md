## Evidence limits

The five merged Atlas PRs are externally inspectable. The public Zenodo preprint (DOI `10.5281/zenodo.23088952`) establishes listed coauthorship; it does not establish peer review or responsibility for the Atlas's full mathematical library. Individual contributions concern source review, citation provenance and repository tooling. Private affiliation correspondence is not published.

Hypermandala's live site and repository are public. Its v0.1.0 release is archived on Zenodo (DOI `10.5281/zenodo.23391082`). The archive establishes a released software version, not adoption, revenue or independent mathematical validation.

Project start dates are distinct from the dates of later localisation features. Notandia's Safari source and iOS Simulator checks do not establish Safari App Store publication or physical-iPhone testing. Store distribution, source release and browser testing remain separate. QCAE implements an existing instrument; AI-assisted multilingual UI work does not imply fluency in every supported language or new psychometric validation. Glossaries document terminology decisions, not independent certification of every translation.

The private LocalEyes CV can include the individual Atlassian exam pass report with credential issuance pending. That report and its candidate-specific URL are excluded from public profiles, general CVs and structured credential records until an appropriate public credential is issued.

Historical contribution totals, organization-wide audience reach and Atlas-wide proof/library totals are not personal achievement metrics. The missing naturalization-map preview remains a source link rather than an unverified substitute. The Italiani veri acknowledgement is verified against the supplied ebook screenshot; other unconfirmed training claims remain outside the public projection.
