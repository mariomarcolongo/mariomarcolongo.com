# Mario Marcolongo public evidence

Read-only public records. No sign-in, key or payment is needed.

## Steps

1. Search: `GET https://mariomarcolongo.com/api/evidence?q=<keywords>&limit=5`
2. Full profile: `GET https://mariomarcolongo.com/api/profile`
3. Résumés: `GET https://mariomarcolongo.com/api/resumes`, optionally with `focus=default`, `technical` or `aiEvaluation`

The same tools are available through MCP at `https://mariomarcolongo.com/mcp`. Details: https://mariomarcolongo.com/agent-service.md
